# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-09

Initial Mubble development release.

### Added

- Original orbital M logo, Mubble branding throughout, locally hosted Manrope, and responsive globe controls.
- Cesium Earth navigation, astronomical sunlight, historical night lights, optional measured terrain, and Natural Earth country boundaries.
- NASA GIBS visible daily mosaics, night infrared, published-date playback, and provider legends.
- NOAA GFS temperature, humidity, pressure, precipitation, and wind fields decoded through Python/ecCodes; wind animation follows model vectors.
- RainViewer radar history, Open-Meteo location search and point forecasts, and NHC advisories with available official tracks.
- Saved places, persistent units/settings, shareable views, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and explicit outage states.
- Unit/browser validation, reproducible setup, GitHub CI, project documentation, MIT license, and third-party attribution.

### Known limits

Daily satellite mosaics are not live geostationary imagery. Radar coverage is regional. Cyclone advisories cover NHC basins. Additional global agencies, uncertainty cones, water vapor, and classified fog/severe-storm products remain on the [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md).
